Strategy rules

The opening range (OR) is the 09:15–09:45 high and low (the first two 15-min candles). Range cap: skip the day if the OR is wider than 100 pts (NIFTY) or 300 pts (SENSEX). ORB · range cap: go LONG the moment price trades above the OR high, SHORT below the OR low, one trade a day. ORB · range cap + 9:50 filter: the 5-min candle ending 09:50 must close beyond the OR (above the high = LONG, below the low = SHORT), entered at that 09:50 close; if it closes inside the range there is no trade. Both: stop at the opposite end of the OR, target 2R (twice the risk), any open trade closed at 14:30. 1 lot per strategy on NIFTY/SENSEX futures, placed automatically by the live engine subject to the risk limits below. The two caps were picked after looking at the backtest data; the 9:50 filter is an unconfirmed lead, still being tested out-of-sample. Risk limits: Rs.3,000 combined daily loss cap, 2-loss breaker per strategy, 10% funds buffer on every order.

Read-only page: it cannot itself place, modify or cancel any order — the automatic engine does that separately, gated by AUTO_TRADING.on and the risk limits above. The P&L on this page is still the signal-replay calculation (index points, assumed fill at the OR edge / 9:50 close, no slippage) — it has not yet been reconciled against actual futures fills. Real order state (fills, rupee P&L, broker responses) is recorded in trade_log.jsonl. ₹ = points × lot size (NIFTY 65, SENSEX 20). Refreshes every 60 seconds from live_state.json.
