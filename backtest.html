How to read this

Treat every number on this page as in-sample and optimistic.
  • The two range caps (NIFTY 100 pts, SENSEX 300 pts) were chosen after looking at this same data, so they flatter the result. Without a cap the strategy is close to break-even (profit factor about 1.05).
  • Plain capped ORB: profit factor about 1.14 (NIFTY, t-stat 1.6) and 1.22 (SENSEX, t-stat 2.1). The edge is small, and all of it sits in normal-sized opening ranges.
  • The 9:50 filter variant trades only about one day in five. Its per-trade quality looks better, but the result disappeared when the confirmation time was moved by five minutes, so it is an unconfirmed lead, and each index has fewer than 180 trades.
  • The backtest assumes a fill exactly at the range edge (or the 9:50 close) with only a fixed cost. Live slippage will be worse. 2026 has been roughly flat for the plain version.
  • That is why the live paper trading exists: to test the rules on data the backtest never saw.
